Rules you can change

The APA 7 preset is a starting point, not a lock-in. Add, edit, or delete rules so the panel matches your school's or advisor's house style. Keep one rule set for your program, adjust it when the requirements change, and reuse it across chapters.

How it works

  1. Keep your rules. Start from the APA 7 preset, then add, edit, or delete rules for your school's or advisor's house style.
  2. Execute. Ask your own AI assistant to apply those rules to your Word file with the chat command Execute.
  3. Check. Ask it to run Check to list the remaining layout gaps. Check reports gaps; it does not silently rewrite your chapter.

What the panel does — and does not do

The panel only stores and manages your rules. The actual edits to your Word file are made by your own AI assistant, using chat commands such as Execute and Check. The panel does not edit Word, and we never receive your .docx.
