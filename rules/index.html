Rules is one Bot Bridge plugin, beside CC Helper, Webcam, Obsidian, and App Bridge. The panel adds, edits, and deletes standing rules. Check only lists gaps. Your own AI assistant does the writing. This panel does not open Word and does not accept a .docx.

What the panel does

  1. Add, edit, and delete rules. Start from the APA 7 preset, or keep a set you write yourself.
  2. Check only lists gaps. It reports what is still missing against the rules you kept. It does not rewrite the document.
  3. Your AI does the editing. Ask your own assistant to apply those rules, for example with the chat commands Execute and Check. The panel never opens the file.
The panel only stores and manages your rules. It does not edit Word, and we never receive your .docx. Any change to a document is made by your own AI assistant.

How a session goes

  1. Keep your rules. Install the panel, then add, edit, or delete rules. The APA 7 preset is only a starting point.
  2. Execute. Ask your own AI assistant to apply those rules. If the work is a Word thesis, that is your file and your assistant — the panel does not open Word.
  3. Check. Ask it to run Check. You get a list of gaps. Check does not silently rewrite the chapter.
