Your Pro key

你的 Pro 激活码

Activated on已绑定的机器

    一张码最多 3 台。解绑一台才能换机。

    One key works on up to 3 machines. Unbind one to free a slot.

    How to activate. In Bot Bridge Rules choose Upgrade Pro and paste the key. The panel registers this PC online. You can also drop the downloaded license.json next to rules.json; the panel will bind this machine the next time it is online.

    在规则面板里选升级 Pro，贴上激活码。面板会联网登记这台电脑。也可以把下载的 license.json 放到 rules.json 旁边，下次联网时会自动绑定。
