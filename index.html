Bot Bridge

Local tools an agent can use on this machine: dispatch Claude Code, keep standing rules, snap one webcam frame, read an Obsidian vault, or inspect the Windows desktop.

Products

Two desktop apps are paid. The other three are free plugins plus a local MCP server.

  • CC Helper Pro · $29/year

    Dispatch a job to Claude Code on your Windows PC and read structured results back in chat. Pro approves pay and submit blockers from your phone, and can push a screenshot through Bark.
  • Ru
    Bot Bridge Rules Early bird · $79/year

    Add, edit, and delete standing rules detailed enough for a layout check to pass, including an APA 7 thesis preset. Your assistant, Claude Code, or a script reads the panel and does the editing.
  • Webcam Bridge Free

    Ask for one photo. A local MCP server opens the camera, returns a single JPEG into the chat, and releases the device.
  • Obsidian Bridge Free

    Point it at a vault path. The agent can read a note, append a work log, and search — on your disk, through a local MCP server.
  • App Bridge Free

    On Windows, list open windows, read the on-screen UI text, and take a screenshot through a local MCP server.
